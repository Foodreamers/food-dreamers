## 1. Who We Are

Food Dreamers is a creative studio specializing in food, offering video production, photography, creative development, and postproduction. Its offering brings together production facilities, food styling resources, props, and artificial intelligence tools.

Published brand statements:

- Home: “The Global Food Storytelling Company”.
- About, desktop: “The Global Food Storytelling Brand.”
- About, mobile: “Creative Studio + AI Lab for the next generation of food brands.”
- Home: “A full-service creative studio specialized in food”.

Proposed copy: **We are a creative studio specializing in food storytelling. We bring together creativity, production, and technology to create content for brands.**

Sources: `app/page.tsx`, `app/about/page.tsx`, `app/layout.tsx`.

## 4. Video and Formats

The Video Book organizes the work into seven categories:

- **TV Ads:** advertising campaigns and stories for the screen.
- **Social Media:** short videos designed to capture attention on social platforms.
- **UGC:** human, spontaneous content that speaks the language of social media.
- **Long Form:** more developed stories about recipes, people, and processes.
- **Shopper:** content focused on retail environments and consumer action.
- **E-commerce:** films focused on products and their details.
- **AI Taste Lab:** exploration of visual possibilities through artificial intelligence.

The Essentials brings together Demo Reel, Studio Tour, and Best Moments. The Home page also lists TikTok, Reel, Shopper, Campaign, TV Ads, Brand Film, and Documentary.

The complete inventory of current titles is included in `inventario-videos.md`. It reflects the local version, with the titles and organization found during this review; it does not imply that they have already been published.

Sources: `app/videobook/page.tsx` and Home.

- Choose the language and primary brand statement: Company, Brand, or Creative Studio + AI Lab.
- Validate “15+ Modular Kitchens” before using it in the final presentation.
- Clarify “1M+ Prop Warehouse”, a statement on the Home page: what it measures is not explained. Do not use it as a sales figure until confirmed.
- Provide, if desired: company history, years of experience, team information, verified geographic reach, campaign results, awards, and testimonials.
- Select case studies and describe the work completed without inventing results.
- Standardize spelling and capitalization across portfolio titles. The reviewed local version already identifies Menú — Isadora, Cápsula Navideña, and Helena’s — Hummus de Pimiento.
